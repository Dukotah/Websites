The Farm

It starts in the dirt.

Glenn Alexander has sustainably farmed more than thirty premier vineyards across Sonoma County. His conviction is simple: exceptional sites and focused farming are the building blocks of every great wine — long before anything reaches a barrel.

That means no chemical shortcuts. The canopy is adjusted by hand for sun and shade, the soils are improved organically, and irrigation is limited or cut entirely to push the vines toward ripe tannins and pure flavor. Clean, fresh, honest wine — the product of hard work.

"Clean, fresh, and pure. The product of hand craft and the product of hard work."
