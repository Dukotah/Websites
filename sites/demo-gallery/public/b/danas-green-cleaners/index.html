Why green

The green part isn't a gimmick.

Most dry cleaning leans on harsh petrochemical solvents. Dana's went 100% organic and eco-friendly instead — better for the clothes, better for the people who wear them and the people who clean them, and better for a town like Sebastopol. Thirty-two years in, that's still the point of the place.

  • 100% organic Eco-friendly, organic cleaning — kinder to your clothes, your skin and the town it's done in.
  • Family-run, 32 years A small, local, family-run shop that's been on Gravenstein Highway for over three decades.
