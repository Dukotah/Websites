The Practitioner

Thirty years of knowing hands.

Marsha Copeland earned her certification at the Amma Institute in San Francisco in 1994, and spent the years since deepening her craft in chiropractic and acupuncture offices and training in modality after modality.

What you feel on the table is all of that experience, brought to the single question of how to help your body let go.
