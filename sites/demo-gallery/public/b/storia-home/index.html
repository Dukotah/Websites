The Story

Curated by a designer's eye.

Storia was founded by interior designer Gina Rachelle, who brings the layered, considered sensibility of her residential studio to the shop's shelves. The name honors the idea that every good object carries a history worth telling.

The shop doubles as a platform for other makers — celebrating their backgrounds and their craft — and reflects the owner's own Italian and Mexican heritage in everything it gathers.

"Storia" — history, story, a tale. Every piece here has one.

A woman-owned home and lifestyle boutique in Sebastopol — a thoughtfully curated shop of furniture, art, textiles, kitchen goods, apothecary and paper, chosen one piece at a time.

It's Italian for history, story or tale. The shop is built to celebrate the story behind each maker and each object — and the owner's own Italian-Mexican heritage.

Storia was founded by interior designer Gina Rachelle, whose residential studio brings the same layered, considered eye to the shop's shelves.

Showroom hours are Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. We also host pop-ups and sidewalk sales — worth checking in before you make the trip.
