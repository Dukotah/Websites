Grain to Glass

Every step, under one roof.

Most "craft" spirits start with alcohol made somewhere else. Ours don't. From the raw grain to the label, it all happens here — that's what grain-to-glass actually means.

  1. 01

    Mill

    Whole grain arrives at the distillery and is milled on site — nothing pre-processed, no neutral spirit bought in.

  2. 02

    Mash & Ferment

    The milled grain is mashed and fermented in-house, where the character of every spirit really begins.

  3. 03

    Distill

    Distilled by hand on our own still by a small, largely female team who know each cut by feel.

  4. 04

    Barrel & Bottle

    Aged, then bottled by hand on site — including barrels we "amplify" through sound in our music-barrel experiment.

Our Story

It started with a family sloe gin.

Timo & Ashby Marshall met doing environmental research on a ship, crossing the world in tight quarters — turns out, perfect training for building a distillery together. Timo's love of his British family's sloe gin was the spark.

They went looking for a small distillery to make it and found something missing instead: true grain-to-glass spirits. So they built it themselves. 14 years on, Spirit Works is led by one of the few female distilling teams in the U.S. and voted Sonoma County's best craft distillery and tasting room again and again.

The full lineup: vodka, gin, the original sloe gin, barrel gin, straight wheat and rye whiskeys, and our four-grain bourbon — all made in the building you're standing in.
