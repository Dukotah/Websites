The Makers

Amsterdam to Sonoma.

Paul Terwilliger grew up in the Bay Area, then wandered through Europe and India before landing in Amsterdam, where he taught himself the art of leather. A young waitress named Jessica caught his eye — he asked her out, then taught her the craft, and together they ran two shops in the heart of the city.

After sixteen years on Amsterdam's Waterloo Plein, the road led back west to the coast where Paul was raised. They set up shop in Sonoma — the same hands, the same standards, now on the Plaza.

"We design our products, cut the leather, do all the stitching, everything. And it's top quality."

— Jessica Zoutendijk
