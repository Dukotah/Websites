The work

Prints, projects, and a way of seeing.

Jerry's photographs range from the local Sonoma Coast to the far corners of the map. The gallery offers them three ways.

01

Fine-art prints

Jerry's landscape and adventure work, offered on archival paper and on metal — for a wall that deserves the real thing.

02

Custom projects & installations

Commissioned prints and larger installations, sized and finished for the space they'll live in.

03

Workshops & expeditions

Seasonal photography workshops, classes and guided expeditions to destinations both local and far afield.

The artist

Jerry Dodrill.

An acclaimed landscape and adventure photographer, Jerry studied fine art at Pacific Union College and assisted the legendary California photographer Galen Rowell before launching his own career in 2000. His work has carried him from the Sonoma Coast to the Sierra, the American Southwest, Iceland and Kyrgyzstan.

The gallery, in Bodega's historic district, is where that work comes home — the best of it, printed large, on paper and on metal.
