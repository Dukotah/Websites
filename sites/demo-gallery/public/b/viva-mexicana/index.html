Since '87

A neighborhood fixture, not a chain.

Viva Mexicana has been on Gravenstein Highway since 1987, and its whole thing has always been traditional Mexican food made with whole, fresh ingredients — the kind of place that grew up with its neighborhood and stayed committed to it. Vegans, gluten-free diners and carne-asada loyalists all leave happy.
