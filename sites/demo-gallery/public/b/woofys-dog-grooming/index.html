Why Woofy's

Small on purpose.

Big grooming shops move a lot of dogs a day, and the animals feel it. Woofy's stays small so it can stay calm — a boutique studio where your dog is the one on the table, not the twentieth in line. That's the whole reason it exists.

  • Small & boutique Not a conveyor belt. A small studio where your dog isn't one of forty on the schedule.
  • Low-stress, always Every step is paced for the animal in the room — calm handling over speed, every time.
  • Dogs & cats, any size Experienced, caring groomers for all breeds and sizes, from the tiny to the enormous.
