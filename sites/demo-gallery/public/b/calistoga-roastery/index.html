The Roast

Roasted here. Loved everywhere.

Coffee is only the start. This corner shop has become a genuine Calistoga landmark — so much so that it's been drawn into the nationally syndicated comic strip Pearls Before Swine again and again since 2016.

Cartoonist Stephan Pastis is a regular, and his original signed storyboards hang right here on the wall. Come for the coffee, stay for the story — and maybe spot yourself in the funny pages.
