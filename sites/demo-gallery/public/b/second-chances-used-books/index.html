The Bookseller

Forty years among the shelves.

Brandy has been a bookseller for four decades and a reader for even longer. She worked at Aardvark Books, managed Copperfield's Used Books for twelve years, and co-owned Mockingbird Books — a whole career of learning which books deserve a second life.

In December 2017 she finally opened a shop of her own. It's the kind of place where the person behind the counter has actually read the spines.

"I was a Virginia Woolf buff in my twenties. It was inevitable I'd choose a room of my own."

— Brandy, owner
