Built by workers, for the people

Fifty years of a store that answers to its neighborhood.

Community Market opened in 1975 as a worker-run cooperative, and it never stopped being one. The people who ring you up, stock the shelves and cut the meat are the same people who own and run the place. There's no owner far away deciding what's best for a quarter — just a store that's stayed accountable to the folks who shop it and the folks who staff it.

"Food for people, not for profit." — the co-op's guiding line, since day one
  • Worker-run The people who run the store own the store. Decisions are made by the folks behind the counter.
  • Values-led Fifty years of putting people over profit — that isn't a slogan we bought, it's how we started in 1975.
  • Two neighborhoods Sebastopol and Santa Rosa. Same co-op, same standards, two front doors close to home.
