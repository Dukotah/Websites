The Story

From 400 square feet to a national list.

Jayme Powers is a Sonoma native who spent years in sparkling-wine production before turning that obsession into a bar of her own. Sigh opened in 2012 in a tiny 400-square-foot room in Vine Alley.

In 2017 it grew to quadruple the size and moved to West Napa Street on the Plaza. 14 years on, it's beloved for one simple thing: making great bubbles feel warm, welcoming and completely unpretentious.

"The exhale after the pop of the cork."
