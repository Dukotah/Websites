The tandoor, the spice, and a Himalayan pinch of salt.

Authentic Indian and Himalayan cooking on Gravenstein Highway — clay-oven tandoori, curries built from scratch, and one of the deeper vegetarian menus you'll find in Sebastopol. Much of it seasoned, as the name suggests, with Himalayan salt.

The menu

What comes out of the kitchen.

Tandoori off the clay oven, curries by the dozen, and a vegetarian section that stands on its own.

From the tandoor

Chicken, lamb and seafood cooked in the clay oven — the smoke and char the whole place is named for.

Curries & masalas

A long list of vegetarian and non-vegetarian curries, built on spices and, where it counts, Himalayan salt.

Vegetarian & vegan

A genuinely deep meat-free menu — this isn't a place where vegetarians pick from two options.

Starters & breads

Samosas, chhoela, fresh naan and roti to start — and to mop up everything that follows.

A few Nepali, Chinese and Pakistani dishes round out the menu too.

The kitchen

Cooked like it means it.

The Himalayan Tandoori & Curry House is exactly what it says: a kitchen that leans on the tandoor and on real spice, and seasons a good part of the menu with Himalayan salt. Vegetarians and meat-eaters both get a full menu here, not a short list of compromises.
