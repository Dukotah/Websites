Dinner at the edge of the continent.

Perched above Jenner where the river empties into the sea, River's End pairs sustainable, locally sourced cooking from Chef Martin Recoder with one of the great sunset views on the California coast. Stay for dinner. Stay for the night.

The Table

Gracious service, inspired dishes.

The kitchen, led by Chef Martin Recoder, builds its menu from sustainable and locally sourced ingredients — food that tastes of this coast, served with the kind of ease that makes a table by the water feel like the only place to be.

Come for the sunset seating and let the room do the rest: the light going gold over the river mouth, the fog rolling in behind it, dinner unhurried.
