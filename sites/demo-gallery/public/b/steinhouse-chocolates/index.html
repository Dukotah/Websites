Brussels, by way of Fourth Street.

Steinhouse is a Belgian chocolate shop in downtown Santa Rosa, run by Eric Dewit — a chocolatier who came over from Brussels. Bars and truffles imported from Belgium, chocolate sauce and popcorn, and a small gourmet pantry gathered from Sonoma to Europe.

The chocolatier

Eric Dewit brought Belgium with him.

Eric moved to the States from Brussels and first opened a Belgian chocolate shop in Mendocino, specializing in chocolate imported from Belgium and truffles made by hand. Steinhouse is where that work landed in Santa Rosa — and the shop recently added a line made exclusively for it by a Belgian chocolatier living in Napa.
